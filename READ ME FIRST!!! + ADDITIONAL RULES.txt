  #000: Invisibility

Invisibility, quite simply, is when one is not quite visible to the naked eye, in the context of a superpower. 
Sometimes this ability is in more subtle forms, such as wearing nondescript clothing 
and having a plain appearance such that one does not stand out from a crowd, 
or more supernatural or superhuman in which the person is not at all visible, and quite possibly incorporeal.

Advantages:
Not being seen allows for privacy in an almost unlimited sense.
Great for introverts trying to recharge their social battery.
Absolutely excellent for people who need to be hidden for some reason, whether natsec (national security) or something else.

Disadvantages:
Gets a little lonely sometimes; if nobody sees you, then how can they be your friend?
Morals tend to loosen if nobody can see you, as do social skills.
Remaining invisible for long periods of time can potentially cause loss of identity and even an unraveling of sanity.
Easy to predict for specialized forensic scientists and mages, especially if invisibility is used in the commission of a crime

The Ugly (only listed for specific superpowers):
Invisibility’s placement at #000 is of course designed to make it a reference point, but the Ugly section won’t always be used. This poses a problem, but here’s the base case at any rate.
